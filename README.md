# SuriQ AI

SuriQ AI helps construction procurement teams catch discrepancies before choosing a supplier. It compares quotations against a bill of materials or quantities and links each finding to the original document so reviewers can check it for themselves.

## Demo

In this demo, a supplier quotes 800 bags of cement against 850 required. SuriQ flags the shortfall and shows the line it came from.

## What reviewers can do

- **Bring purchase documents together.** Review a bill of materials or quantities alongside up to five supplier quotations. SuriQ accepts PDFs, Excel workbooks, and Word documents, and uses OCR for scanned PDFs.
- **Inspect and resolve findings.** Check differences in specifications and quantities, verify quoted arithmetic, and jump to the source page or cell. Keep corrections and review decisions with the procurement batch.
- **Compare offers on the same basis.** See prices, scope coverage, and terms such as VAT and delivery side by side. SuriQ explains when totals are incomparable, for example when an offer covers only part of the purchase or uses a different currency.
- **Share the review.** Export a PDF or CSV, an annotated copy of an uploaded Excel workbook, or a PDF Abstract of Bids.

Built with Next.js, TypeScript, FastAPI, and SQLite.
